# Executability, feasibility, and simplicity

**Load when:** the target proposes a process, architecture, plan, workflow, interface, acceptance obligation, or implementation approach.

**Retrieve:** existing interfaces/capabilities, prerequisites, producers/consumers, ordering and constraints, affected operational boundaries, and the proposed verification approach.

**Check:** circular dependencies; missing prerequisites; impossible or invalid ordering; unsupported capabilities; operations with no viable verification; unnecessary infrastructure/state/dependencies/abstractions; and complexity disproportionate to the requirement. Evaluate a simpler or safer option only if it meets the same governing constraints and required outcomes.

**Report:** cite the concrete interface, producer, dependency, constraint, or missing capability. Distinguish demonstrated infeasibility from an assumption that needs validation. Do not report mere design preference or an alternative that violates required constraints.
