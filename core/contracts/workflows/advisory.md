# Advisory workflow

Use advisory for graph-free repository research and bounded architecture or product proposals. The controller owns scope, source-authority discovery, and any user-facing decision request; researchers and architects return evidence and options, never approval.

An accepted decision is an explicit human choice bound to its exact scope and governing sources. Acceptance does not approve implementation. Promotion requires a separately approved plan bound to the accepted snapshot and current source bytes. Without a conformant validator, work may remain non-authoritative research, but no accepted decision or plan approval may be claimed.
