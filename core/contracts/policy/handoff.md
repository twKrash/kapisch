# Handoff policy

A handoff carries only bounded task context, exact scope, required authority, declared effect ceiling, and evidence needed by the receiving role. The sender owns the assignment and requested work; the receiver owns its returned judgment or result; the controller persists exact returned bytes and binds producer, target, revision, and digest.

Delegation cannot grant reviewer authority or exceed the initiating authority. External-write and destructive effects are blocked until an explicit safe-intent and reconciliation protocol exists. Preserve the one-writer rule and ordered dependency chain; never treat a handoff report as a human decision or validator result.
