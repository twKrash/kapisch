# Risk policy

Risk is independent of implementation complexity. Classify impact from repository evidence and stated consequences; do not lower risk because a task looks easy, a cheaper profile is selected, or a worker proposes a narrower label.

High-risk work requires the core-defined role and tier floor and deep independent review. Review depth may increase with risk; risk alone does not invent a final-readiness requirement. Unknown impact that could change authority or safety remains unresolved and blocks the affected gate.
