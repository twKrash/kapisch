# Semantic consistency and invariants

**Load when:** the target has related claims, definitions, requirements, examples, or invariants.

**Retrieve:** governing requirements, relevant terminology and definitions, related artifacts, and the claimed enforcement locations/evidence.

**Check:** contradictions; conflicting requirements or invariants; terminology drift; hidden assumptions; ambiguous definitions; and semantic inconsistencies among the reviewed artifacts, implementation, tests, and observed behavior. Do not choose an interpretation when authority or meaning is unresolved.

**Report:** cite both sides of a material inconsistency and any governing invariant. State the affected scope and plausible consequence. Give explicit attention to ownership, eligibility, retention, and scope terms when they change who or what a mutation may affect, and to thresholds that change when it occurs. If a governing source is not supplied, ask for the source/owner decision; do not infer that no external authority exists. If evidence supports ambiguity but not a specific defect, name the plausible interpretations and report an unresolved ambiguity rather than asserting a defect or silently choosing an interpretation.
