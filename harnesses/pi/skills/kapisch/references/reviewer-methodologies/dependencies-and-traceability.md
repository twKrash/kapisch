# Dependencies and traceability

**Load when:** the target depends on, produces, or is consumed by other artifacts, domains, implementation, or verification.

**Retrieve:** governing requirements, identified producers and consumers, dependency and sequencing statements, relevant code/tests, domain boundaries, acceptance criteria, and verification obligations.

**Check:** missing/incorrect dependencies and cross-domain effects; requirement-to-design and design-to-plan traceability; plan-to-implementation coverage; implementation-to-test/observed-verification coverage; explicit producers for consumed artifacts; and each acceptance/verification obligation. Check whether downstream consumers can rely on the claimed output and lifecycle.

**Report:** show the evidenced edge, its source and consumer, or the missing edge and consequence. Distinguish missing documentation from a proven missing dependency; do not assume an artifact or producer exists without evidence.
