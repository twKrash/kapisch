# Authority and ownership

**Load when:** the target describes a decision, source of truth, status, approval, generated record, responsible producer, data owner, or permission boundary.

**Retrieve:** applicable authority hierarchy, provenance and status of each source, accountable owner/producer, relevant human decisions, consumer expectations, and data/resource ownership and authorization facts.

**Check:** conflicting sources of truth; unclear or duplicate ownership; incorrect decision attribution; proposals represented as approved or implemented; recommendations treated as human authority; derived artifacts contradicting governing sources; and operations acting on data/resources outside an authorized owner boundary.

**Report:** cite source, status, attribution, owner/producer, and permission evidence. Identify unresolved authority conflicts without resolving them for a human. For a mutation, also load Mutation and Side-effect Justification.
